Model assumptions

Arrivals and service durations follow gamma distributions with the stated means. CV = standard deviation / mean; higher CV creates more uneven arrivals or longer service-time tails. The doctor CV applies within each patient type; mixing 5-minute prescriptions and 30-minute admissions adds further variability. Two clerks and seven doctors serve patients in FIFO order, starting the next patient immediately upon becoming free. The simulation uses a 30-day warm-up. The default CVs were calibrated across 12 year-long runs to produce waiting queues near 30 and 40; the exercise does not determine these CVs uniquely. Queue lengths and waits are never imposed.
